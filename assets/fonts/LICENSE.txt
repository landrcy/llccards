downloads) could not be found. These alternatives were checked:

  Also downloaded and rejected
  ----------------------------
  MsPain ("lamah") by Anubis & einbein, 2008, from DaFont.
  License text: "This font is free, do not resell."
  Looked more pixel-fuzzy, but a bit harder to read at small sizes.

  NoobPaint Font by Yoohoo, from https://yoohoosearch.neocities.org/font/
  Stated "free to use." Offers TTF, WOFF and SVG. Missing digit glyphs,
  so it cannot set ATK/DEF numbers.
