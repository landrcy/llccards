HOW TO PLAY
  • Both players start with 4000 LP. Hit 0 and you lose. Run out of cards to draw and you lose.
  • Each turn: DRAW, MAIN, BATTLE, END. The first player skips the first draw and cannot attack turn 1.
  • MAIN: play 1 monster per turn, face-up in ATTACK or DEFENSE mode (no face-down monsters). 5-star monsters need 1 tribute. Cast spells, or set spells and traps face-down.
  • Monsters cannot attack the turn they are played (STICK RUNNER can). Change a monster's position once per turn.
  • BATTLE: ATK vs ATK, the weaker one is destroyed and its owner takes the difference. ATK vs DEF, the defender is destroyed if ATK is higher; if DEF is higher, the attacker's owner takes the difference.
  • No monsters on the other side? Attack directly.
  • Set traps fire on the opponent's turn when they attack. Only 1 trap per turn; WHOOPS! costs 300 LP and only stops monsters with 1500+ ATK. Max 2 copies of each trap in a deck.
  • Too many cards at the end of your turn? Your hand turns red: tap a card, then tap it again to discard it.
