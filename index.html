    HOW TO PLAY
    • Both players start with 4000 LP. Hit 0 and you lose. No cards left to draw? You lose 500 LP each time instead.
    • Each turn: DRAW, MAIN, BATTLE, END. The first player skips the first draw and cannot attack turn 1.
    • MAIN: play 1 monster per turn, face-up in ATTACK or DEFENSE mode (no face-down monsters). 5-6 star monsters need 1 tribute, 7+ stars need 2. Cast spells, or set spells and traps face-down.
    • EQUIPS and CURSES stay face-up in your spell/trap row, stuck to a monster. If that monster leaves, they go too.
    • FIELD spells sit in the middle and change stats for BOTH players. A new field replaces the old one.
    • COMBO cards need the right monsters on your side. COUNTER traps cancel a spell as it is cast.
    • Monsters cannot attack the turn they are played (STICK RUNNER can). Change a monster's position once per turn.
    • BATTLE: ATK vs ATK, the weaker one is destroyed and its owner takes the difference. ATK vs DEF, the defender is destroyed if ATK is higher; if DEF is higher, the attacker's owner takes the difference.
    • No monsters on the other side? Attack directly.
    • Set traps fire on the opponent's turn: when they attack, summon or cast. Only 1 trap per player per turn. A deck holds max 3 of a card, 2 of a trap or Super Rare, 1 of a Secret Rare.
    • Too many cards at the end of your turn? Your hand turns red: tap a card, then tap it again to discard it.
